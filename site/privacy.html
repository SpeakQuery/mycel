Privacy

Mycel, by SpeakQuery · applies to the npm package, the installers and the Claude Desktop extension.

In short

Mycel runs on your computer. It has no account, no analytics and no telemetry. We (SpeakQuery) receive nothing from it.

What stays on your computer

  • Your Mycel config (~/.mycel/mycel.yaml), the flight log of tool calls, savings counts, backups of the app settings it changed, and large results kept as ref:// files.
  • API keys: in the macOS Keychain or your Linux keyring, otherwise in ~/.mycel/secrets.env (readable only by you). The AI model never sees them; Mycel replaces them in anything it returns.
  • Sign-in tokens for services that use OAuth (for example Canva or Notion), stored locally and refreshed automatically.

When Mycel connects to the internet

  • To the tools you connect. Calls go to the MCP servers in your config, as they would without Mycel.
  • Update check. At most once a day it asks the public npm registry (registry.npmjs.org) for the latest version number, and downloads the new version from npm if there is one. Nothing about you or your usage is sent. Turn it off with update: { auto: false }.
  • Only if you set them up: an OpenTelemetry endpoint for traces, a model API for mycel_ask, or other agents over A2A.
  • The installers download Node.js from nodejs.org (checked against its published SHA-256) when you do not have it, and Mycel from npm.

Removing it

mycel uninstall restores your apps' settings from before Mycel, removes its keychain entries and deletes ~/.mycel. In Claude Desktop you can also ask Claude to undo the Mycel setup, then remove the extension in Settings → Extensions.
